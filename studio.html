How do I move the 3D fountain model?

Drag the model to rotate it, right-drag to pan, and scroll to zoom in or out. The arrow pad moves the view horizontally and vertically, while Auto-rotate and Reset view give you a quick way to inspect every angle.

Can I change the colour, finish and water tint?

Yes. The stone colour, water tint and finish swatches update the 3D fountain instantly, so you can compare options such as Carrara White, Granite Noir, honed or polished surfaces and clear or coloured water before you decide.

What happens when I press Send My Design Brief?

A WhatsApp message opens with your selected design, stone colour, finish, water tint and scale already filled in. Nothing is sent automatically — you can review and edit the message, then press send inside WhatsApp.

Can I use my own fountain idea instead of a listed design?

Yes. Choose Bespoke - made around my idea from the design dropdown, then describe your idea and share a photograph of the space in your WhatsApp message. Our team will review it and suggest the best way forward.
